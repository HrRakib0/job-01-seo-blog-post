Healthy Nutrition & Meal Planning Guide for Beginners

Published as an internal reference for the Daily Fitness Routine for Beginners series.

1. The Foundation of Workout Nutrition

Your fitness routine is only as effective as the nutrients fueling your body. Proper nutrition provides the energy needed to power through daily exercises and supplies the amino acids required to repair and rebuild muscle tissue.

2. Essential Pillars of Balanced Eating

  • Quality Proteins: Eggs, chicken breast, lentils, beans, and fish help repair muscles after workouts.
  • Complex Carbohydrates: Oats, brown rice, whole wheat roti, and sweet potatoes offer long-lasting sustained energy.
  • Healthy Fats: Olive oil, nuts, and seeds support joint health and hormone balance.
  • Adequate Hydration: Drink at least 2.5 to 3 liters of water daily, especially before and after your workouts.

3. Pre-Workout and Post-Workout Snacks

A light pre-workout snack (such as a banana with a few almonds) 30 minutes before your workout keeps your energy stable. Within an hour after working out, consume a balanced meal with protein and healthy carbs to facilitate quick recovery.
